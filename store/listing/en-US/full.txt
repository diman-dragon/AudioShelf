AudioShelf is a simple player for the audiobooks and music already stored on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no subscriptions: the app works offline and your files and data never leave your phone.

A LIBRARY BUILT FROM YOUR FOLDER
• Pick a folder with audio and the app scans it and all its subfolders.
• A folder of chapters becomes a book; single files become tracks and albums. Single-file books (.m4b) are recognized on their own, and "CD1", "CD2" folders are merged into one book.
• Covers come from the files; when there is none, a neat icon is shown: a closed book, an open book once you start listening, or a disc.
• Filter "All / Books / Albums", search by title, author, narrator and year, and sorting.
• Your own tags: title, author, narrator, year and type. Your files are never changed.
• The library refreshes only when you want it to, with a button. Scanning runs in the background and shows its progress.

A PLAYER THAT REMEMBERS
• Remembers your place in every book and resumes 5 seconds earlier, so you can pick up the thread.
• Bookmarks anywhere in a book and a chapter list one tap away.
• Whole-book progress and time left.
• Skip 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, your choice. Speed from 0.75× to 2×.
• Sleep timer: after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or at the end of the chapter. It counts only while playing.
• Books and albums have their own modes: "continuous" and "pause after chapter" for books; repeat, repeat track and shuffle for albums.
• Smooth fades between album tracks, instant switching between book chapters.

SOUND TO YOUR TASTE (PREMIUM)
• 10-band equalizer from 31 Hz to 16 kHz.
• Presets: Voice, Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, Electronic, Bass, Treble.
• Set it for everything at once, or separately for a chosen book or album.
• Loudness normalization: the app measures your files in the background and evens out the volume during playback. The files themselves are not changed.

BACKGROUND PLAYBACK
• Control from the notification shade and the lock screen, with cover art.
• Pauses when headphones are unplugged and during calls.

PREMIUM — ONE PAYMENT, FOREVER
The equalizer and loudness normalization are part of Premium: a one-time purchase, no subscription. Everything else is free and ad-free. If you like the app, you can buy the author a tea — it is optional and unlocks nothing.

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• Light (sand) and dark (AMOLED) themes, English and Russian.
• Everything you set up stays on your device.
• Access only to the folder you choose.

Supports MP3, M4A, M4B, AAC, OGG, Opus, FLAC, WAV and other formats your Android can play.
